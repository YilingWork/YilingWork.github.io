Privacy Policy

e-PI RA Portal · Last updated 10 October 2026

What this site is

The e-PI RA Portal is an internal tool for the research assistants of the e-PI acceptance study (Indonesian and Vietnamese questionnaires). It is used to record refusals, on-site questionnaire checks and data entry. Only people who have received a personal invitation from the principal investigator can sign in.

Information we receive from Google

When you sign in with Google, the site receives only your basic profile: your name, email address and Google account ID. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • To confirm that you are an invited member of the study team.
  • To record which team member entered each record, as required by the study protocol.

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Where it is stored

  • Your name, email address and account ID, and the records you enter, are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet that only the principal investigator can access.
  • Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you do not have to sign in every time. It expires after 14 days, and you can remove it at any time with “Sign out”.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept until the end of the study and then deleted. Study records are kept according to the study protocol approved by the Institutional Review Board.

Questions about this policy or requests to remove your account: Yiling Tsai, Principal Investigator, tyilingteam@gmail.com.
